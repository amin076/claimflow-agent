# Product Vision

## Vision

Help document-heavy teams move from messy evidence to trustworthy action without hiding uncertainty or removing human accountability.

## Product promise

ClaimFlow AI organises incoming evidence, proposes a structured case, explains where each fact came from, and asks for human help when confidence or authority is insufficient.

## Differentiation

1. Evidence lineage rather than unreferenced extraction.
2. Cross-document contradiction detection.
3. Hybrid agents plus deterministic guardrails.
4. Human review as a designed state, not an exception.
5. Auditable case transitions.
6. Communication drafts that require approval.

## Non-goals for the hackathon

- replacing a claim assessor;
- deciding coverage, liability, or payment;
- integrating with a real company production system;
- processing real customer data;
- building a generic chat interface;
- promising perfect handwriting recognition.

## Future direction

After the MVP, the architecture could support configurable case schemas, organisation-specific policies, email and CRM connectors, privacy controls, benchmark evaluation, role-based access, and multilingual voice interactions.
