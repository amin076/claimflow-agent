# Problem Statement

## Target environment

Restoration, claims, field-service, and similar operations receive evidence through email, mobile photographs, PDFs, handwritten forms, invoices, and reports. The inputs are often incomplete, duplicated, inconsistent, or difficult to read.

## Core problem

Staff must manually turn fragmented evidence into a reliable case record before work can be scheduled or escalated. This creates delay, rework, transcription errors, and weak traceability.

## Why existing OCR is insufficient

OCR answers “what text might be visible?” The operational problem also requires:

- document classification;
- cross-document reconciliation;
- uncertainty and confidence handling;
- source evidence for every important fact;
- business-rule validation;
- human approval for consequential steps;
- a recommended, auditable next action.

## Target user

The MVP targets a case coordinator or operations administrator who triages incoming work. It does not target an insurance assessor making coverage or liability decisions.

## Hypothesis

If mixed-quality evidence can be converted into an evidence-linked draft case with explicit uncertainty, then coordinators can spend less time transcribing and more time resolving the few facts that genuinely need judgment.

## Prototype success measures

- time from upload to review-ready case;
- percentage of required fields proposed;
- accuracy on the synthetic benchmark;
- percentage of uncertain fields correctly routed to review;
- number of contradictions surfaced;
- correction effort before final approval.

No productivity claim will be presented as proven without measured evidence.
