# Team Responsibilities

Ownership should be confirmed before the official start and adjusted to actual skills and availability.

## Amin — Product and agent/backend lead

- product scope and architecture;
- domain schemas and evidence model;
- Google ADK workflow;
- Gemini and Google Cloud integration;
- deterministic validation;
- deployment and technical explanation;
- integration review.

## Behzad — Proposed UX, review, and demo lead

- upload and case-workspace user experience;
- confidence and evidence presentation;
- human-review controls;
- synthetic demo-case preparation;
- usability and responsive testing;
- demo recording and pitch support.

If Behzad's strengths are primarily business or presentation, frontend ownership should move to Amin or another teammate and Behzad should own workflow research, value measurement, sample-case design, Devpost, video, and pitch.

## Shared responsibilities

- define acceptance criteria;
- review pull requests;
- run end-to-end smoke tests;
- maintain the issue board;
- document limitations;
- present a shared understanding during Q&A.

## Collaboration rules

- one accountable owner per issue;
- no direct implementation pushes to `main`;
- small pull requests;
- no secrets or real customer data;
- conflicts resolved by the MVP acceptance criteria and demo reliability;
- every blocked task gets a cut, fallback, or explicit deadline.
